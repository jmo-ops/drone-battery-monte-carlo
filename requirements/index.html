    Requirements

    This page captures the system/model requirements for the Drone Battery Monte Carlo model and how each requirement is evaluated.

    REQ-001 — Mission Energy Use Under Variable Conditions

    Requirement

    The model shall estimate battery energy draw per mission as a function of payload weight, flight distance, ambient temperature, and wind conditions.

    Assumptions / Constraints

    • The drone follows a defined mission profile.
    • Empty drone weight is fixed at 50 lb for all simulation runs.
    • Cruise speed is fixed at 25 mph.
    • Payload varies from 0–5 lb.
    • Wind, temperature, payload, and distance factor are sampled stochastically.
    • Distance is modeled as:
    • nominal mission distance = 15 mi
    • distance factor = 0.9–1.1 nominal, or 1.0–1.1 in extreme mode
    • Battery performance is affected by:
    • baseline power draw
    • fixed drone-weight penalty
    • payload penalty
    • wind penalty
    • cold-weather power increase
    • cold-weather effective-capacity reduction
    • A 10% safety reserve is required.

    Question(s) Answered

    • How much energy does a mission consume under varying operating conditions?
    • Which operational factors most affect mission battery draw?

    Primary Variables

    • Payload Weight
    • Flight Distance
    • Ambient Temperature
    • Wind Speed
    • Battery Configuration

    Method

    • Monte Carlo analysis
    • Randomly generate mission/environmental inputs
    • Compute:
    • mission distance
    • power draw
    • energy required
    • effective capacity
    • remaining energy
    • mission success/failure versus reserve threshold

    Outputs / Acceptance Evidence

    • Energy used per mission (Wh)
    • Effective battery capacity (Wh)
    • Remaining energy after mission (Wh, %)
    • Success/failure against reserve threshold
    • Distribution statistics:
    • mean remaining energy
    • 5th percentile remaining energy
    • 95th percentile remaining energy

    Validation Data Source

    • Battery test data
    • Engineering assumptions for power and temperature sensitivity
    • Simulation consistency checks

    Analysis Deliverable

    • Plot/table of remaining energy distribution
    • Summary statistics for mission energy use
    • Sensitivity discussion for payload, distance, temperature, and wind

    REQ-002 — Mission Feasibility and Deliveries per Charge

    Requirement

    The model shall determine whether a candidate battery can complete a mission profile and estimate the number of missions per charge under variable operating conditions.

    Assumptions / Constraints

    • The drone follows a fixed cruise speed of 25 mph.
    • Empty drone weight is fixed at 50 lb.
    • Payload varies from 0–5 lb.
    • Mission distance varies around the nominal 15 mi baseline.
    • Wind and temperature vary according to the selected Monte Carlo mode.
    • A mission is considered successful only if energy required does not exceed usable battery energy after reserve.
    • The model reports missions per charge and deliveries per full charge using average mission energy and average usable capacity.

    Question(s) Answered

    • Can the battery complete the mission while maintaining reserve?
    • How many deliveries can be completed per charge?
    • How much reserve margin remains after mission completion?

    Primary Variables

    • Battery Configuration
    • Payload Weight
    • Flight Distance
    • Ambient Temperature
    • Wind Speed

    Method

    • Monte Carlo analysis
    • Run repeated randomized mission trials for each battery candidate
    • Compare mission energy demand against effective capacity after reserve
    • Aggregate results across all trials

    Outputs / Acceptance Evidence

    • Mission success probability
    • Average energy used per mission (Wh)
    • Missions per charge
    • Deliveries per charge
    • Remaining energy distribution
    • Comparative battery performance under nominal and extreme conditions

    Validation Data Source

    • End-to-end simulation behavior
    • Internal consistency checks
    • Comparison of expected depletion behavior versus model output

    Analysis Deliverable

    • Mission success summary by battery
    • Table/chart of missions per charge
    • Comparison of remaining energy distributions across batteries

    REQ-003 — Comparative Battery Evaluation

    Requirement

    The model shall compare candidate battery configurations and determine which configuration provides better mission margin and missions-per-charge performance under common operating conditions.

    Assumptions / Constraints

    • All battery candidates are evaluated using the same mission/environment input framework.
    • The drone platform is assumed constant:
    • empty drone weight = 50 lb
    • same mission speed and reserve threshold
    • Chemistry differences are represented through battery parameters, including:
    • nominal capacity
    • baseline power draw
    • payload penalty
    • wind penalty
    • cold-capacity penalty
    • cold-power penalty
    • The current model compares battery candidates for mission performance, but does not yet implement recharge-cycle degradation or true end-of-life lifecycle modeling.

    Question(s) Answered

    • Which battery retains more post-mission energy?
    • Which battery supports more missions per charge?
    • How do candidate batteries differ under nominal and extreme conditions?

    Primary Variables

    • Battery Candidate / Battery Configuration

    Method

    • Monte Carlo analysis
    • Execute the same set of randomized mission trials for each battery candidate
    • Compare aggregated summary statistics between batteries

    Outputs / Acceptance Evidence

    • Mean remaining energy (%)
    • 5th and 95th percentile remaining energy
    • Average mission energy use (Wh)
    • Missions per charge
    • Success rate
    • Delta comparison between candidate batteries

    Validation Data Source

    • Battery specification assumptions
    • Comparative simulation results
    • Engineering review of parameter realism

    Analysis Deliverable

    • Comparison chart/table per battery:
    • average remaining energy
    • percentile bands
    • average Wh per mission
    • missions per charge
    • success rate
    • Nominal vs extreme comparison summary

    Limitation / Future Requirement

    FUTURE-REQ — Trips Before Battery End-of-Life (EOL)

    The current model does not yet implement true battery lifecycle degradation across recharge cycles. A future enhancement should add: - cycle-dependent battery degradation - capacity fade over recharge count - performance degradation versus age/use - total trips before battery end-of-life

    This would support a future requirement such as:

    The model shall determine the total number of trips achievable for each candidate battery before end-of-life (EOL) using a lifecycle degradation model.

    Notes

    • The current model explicitly assumes a 50 lb empty drone weight.
    • The model currently evaluates single-mission performance and charge-level mission throughput.
    • The model does not yet calculate long-term battery EOL unless lifecycle degradation logic is added.
    • Battery chemistry is represented indirectly through configuration parameters rather than a standalone chemistry equation.
